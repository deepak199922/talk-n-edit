## What Talk-n-Edit does not do

- **It does not bypass copy protection.** It doesn't remove or get around DRM, encryption, paywalls or other technical protection measures. Material protected that way isn't downloaded. It sees what a normal visitor to a public page sees.
- **It does not remove ownership information** such as watermarks or credits on its own. Hiding a logo with a blur (the masks feature) is something you ask for, on material you have the right to edit.
- **It does not collect your data.** Talk-n-Edit has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry. Talk-n-Edit itself sends nothing anywhere: your videos, transcripts and edits are stored on your computer, and downloads go directly from the site to your computer. If you ask it to use your browser's sign-in (`cookiesFromBrowser`), the cookies are read on your computer for that download only and are not stored or sent anywhere else by Talk-n-Edit.
- **It does not make decisions for you.** Claude proposes edits; you choose what to keep and what to publish.

The Claude app you use with Talk-n-Edit is a separate product from Anthropic, under Anthropic's own terms and privacy policy. What you type to Claude, and what Talk-n-Edit's tools return to it (for example transcripts, video details and preview frames), is handled by the Claude app under those terms.

## Results and performance vary

- **AI output can be wrong.** Transcripts, captions, translations, language detection, chosen moments and suggested hooks or post captions can contain mistakes. Review everything before you publish it.
- **Speed and quality depend on your setup:** your computer (processor, memory, graphics), your internet connection, the source video, and the sites you download from. Times given anywhere in this project are examples, not promises. Older computers are slower.
- **Websites change.** Downloads can stop working at any time when a site changes, limits or blocks access. That isn't a defect Talk-n-Edit can guarantee against.
- **Keep your own backups** of anything important. Talk-n-Edit never deletes or changes your original files, but no software is free of bugs.
