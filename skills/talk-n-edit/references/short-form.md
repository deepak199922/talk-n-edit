# Picking and shaping short-form clips

## Score every candidate (1–5 each, keep the top N)

| Criterion | What scores 5 |
| --- | --- |
| Hook | First sentence is a claim, a surprising number, a question, or a strong opinion |
| Stands alone | No "as I said", no unexplained "he/that/it" referring to earlier context |
| Payoff | Ends on the answer, the punchline or the lesson, not on a transition |
| Energy | Emotion, contrast, a story, a contrarian take; not a list of caveats |
| Length fit | Reels/Shorts/TikTok: 20–60 s after silence removal (90 s max) |

Prefer candidates spread across the video, not three from the same minute.

## Shaping

- Start on the strongest sentence, even if the topic was introduced earlier; drop warm-ups ("So, um, basically…").
- End right after the payoff line. A 1–2 s TitleCard ("Follow for part 2", "Full video on YouTube") is optional.
- HookText: 3–8 words, a promise or a tension, not a summary of the clip. Examples: "Nobody tells you this about EMIs", "I wasted 2 years doing this".
- If the best sentence is in the middle of a long answer, it is fine to cut from two ranges of the same speaker (two clips in the timeline) as long as the meaning is preserved.
- Use `word-pop` captions for fast talkers or hype content, `karaoke-highlight` for most talking-head clips, `clean-subtitle` for 16:9.

## Deliverable notes for the user

For each clip give: file name, length, the hook, and a suggested post caption (1–2 lines) with 3–5 hashtags in the video's language.
